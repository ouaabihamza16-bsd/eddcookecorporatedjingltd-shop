About

Meet Edd Cooke

Edd Cooke Corporate DJing Ltd is the corporate events company of Wolverhampton DJ Edd Cooke, set up in September 2023.

Edd has been playing club nights in Wolverhampton since the early 2010s. In 2022 he DJ'd in the Wolves Fan Zone at Molineux, warming up the crowd before home games.

Corporate crowds are mixed: different ages, different tastes and a schedule to keep to. Edd brings his club and crowd experience to business events and plays what works for the room, from quiet background music during dinner to a full dance floor at the end of the night.
